# Anna Ready decor refresh

Included: filled and line-only Korea maps (with Jeju, Ulleungdo and Dokdo), six matching ornaments, eight icons, contact envelope, booklet back-cover texture, phone hero collage, Adobe wordmark, Hancom Office product icon, and UC seal sourced from UCSC.

PNG dimensions match the original brief. Ornaments and icons include editable SVGs with a subtle grain mask. The neutral sparkle serves as the AI-tools icon. The generated icon drafts that added unwanted colours or objects were rejected.

Limitations: The maps are illustrative, not GIS-verified, and the filled version retains some generated edge colour variation and a pale land fill. Use the line-only version if those do not suit the site. The vector marks use subtle grain rather than a fully hand-inked brush finish. The UCSC-sourced seal is the University of California seal; its original is 195x198 pixels, resampled to 473x480, not a high-resolution original. The hero keeps the original black-and-cream tones as requested by its specific brief.
