# Manifesto

**Status:** approved by Anna, 15 Nov 2026.

## Intent

> **One press run: two inks on one sheet of paper, so the teaching work is the only full colour on the page.**

## Rules

Every later change cites one of these by number.

1. **Two inks, one sheet.** Paper, aubergine, lime. Lime is punctuation. Nothing else in the site's own design carries colour.
2. **The work is the loudest thing.** One artefact per project, shown large, on one mount. The site's design stays quieter than the work it frames.
3. **Loud once, then quiet.** One standout per screen. A loud moment is followed by space.
4. **Print where people look, standard where people click.** Hero, covers and headings are expressive. Navigation, buttons, filters, contact and the language switch behave the way every site does.
5. **Both languages at full weight.** Korean is designed, with the same hierarchy as English. It is never a translation layer.
6. **Ink moves like ink.** Every animation is a printing event: a plate dropping into registration, ink laying down, a sheet feeding through the rollers. No generic fade-ups, no spinners. Motion never delays content and is off under reduced motion. *(Added 15 Nov 2026, approved by Anna.)*
7. **Texture is the substrate, not a sticker.** Grain lives in the paper and in every ink field. Riso ornaments appear at architectural scale, one per band, cropped by the band edge. Work samples are never textured or recoloured. *(Added 15 Nov 2026, approved by Anna.)*
